About

Modern studio craft with an artist-first approach

Sharrix Studios is positioned as a contemporary music and audio studio focused on expressive production, polished sound engineering, and memorable creative execution. The brand direction is based on public references connecting Sharrix Studios with Siddhant Kandalkar.
